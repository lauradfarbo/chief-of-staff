# Leadership observation

The user's goal is to grow their communication and soft skills in small weekly steps. Each week this skill notes one habit in the user's own speech from recorded meetings and suggests one small action. One item. Easy to read. Their own development, nothing else.

## Off until the user turns it on

Do not run until the user has said yes in the chat to the scope below. Ask once, in plain words, listing what the skill will and will not do. Include this reminder: **"Check that recording and analyzing meetings is allowed under your company's rules and that participants know they are recorded."** If the user says "pause leadership notes" or "stop leadership notes", stop until they say to restart. Record the state (`leadership notes: on/off`) in `preferences.md`.

## Scope

**Eligible:** internal meetings with four or more people where the ~~meeting notes include a transcript with speaker names.

**Never used:**
- 1:1s with anyone
- meetings with customers, vendors or anyone outside the user's organization
- hiring, HR, legal and personnel topics
- any meeting the user marks private
- in-person meetings with no recording

**Optional second source, off by default:** the user's own written messages in public ~~chat channels, for wording only. Never direct messages. Turn it on only if the user asks.

## Weekly run

Run at the day and time set in `preferences.md` (default Friday, mid-afternoon), or when the user asks.

1. Check the user has turned it on and has not paused it.
2. List eligible meetings from the past 7 days and find their notes in ~~meeting notes or ~~docs, including notes shared with the user.
3. Keep a meeting only if its notes include a transcript with speaker names. If notes are summary only, skip it.
4. Require at least two eligible meetings where the user spoke. If not, send nothing. Do not send a "no observation" message.
5. Measure the behaviors below, for the user's speech only.
6. Pick one. Write the message. Update the log. Send it to the user themselves only.

## Behaviors

Count the user's own speech only.

- **Softeners and tag questions.** "Just", "sorry to", "I think maybe", "does that make sense", and tags such as "okay?" or "right?" on a statement or request.
  Action: state it as a sentence and stop. "We are going with option two." in place of "Let's just go with option two, okay?"
- **Airtime.** The user's share of spoken words in meetings of four or more where they were not the main presenter. Report the percentage and number of attendees only.
  Action: after you ask a question, let two other people answer before you add.
- **Invitations.** How often the user asked for others' views.
  Action: ask one open question before stating your view in the next meeting.
- **Framing.** Problems raised without a proposed next step, or solutions offered before the problem was discussed.
  Action: pair each problem with one option, or ask what others see before proposing.
- **Overlap.** Times the user started speaking before another speaker finished, when the transcript shows it plainly. Skip it if timing is unclear.
  Action: count to two before you reply.

## Choosing the one

- Choose the behavior with the clearest evidence.
- Do not repeat the same behavior two weeks running, unless the count is moving the wrong way.
- Week one is a baseline. Report the count and say it is the starting point.
- When a behavior has a count and a previous count exists, add the change on one line. Say it plainly when it improves.
- Say what the transcript shows. It is a signal, not a verdict, and the user is the judge. If the evidence is thin, pick something else or send nothing.

## Message

Sixty words at most.

```
Leadership note, week of [date].
Noticed: [the behavior in plain words, with a count or a short phrase of their own].
Try next week: [one action, one sentence].
[Optional: Last time [N]. This week [N].]
```

Plain tone. No scores, grades or comparisons to anyone. No praise filler.

## Log

Keep `observation-log.md` in the profile folder. Tell the user once when they opt in where it is stored and who else may have access to that folder, and offer to move it to a private location. One row per week: date, meeting titles, behavior, count, action, and the user's feedback if given. Keep 12 weeks and delete older rows. Do not keep transcript text beyond the user's own phrases of six words or fewer.

## Privacy rules

- The user's own speech only. Never name another person. Others appear only as aggregate numbers, such as "others".
- Never quote or paraphrase anyone else.
- Never put an observation in the briefing, a shared doc or any file other than the log. Send it only to the user.
- Never send it to anyone else.
- Do not copy transcripts out of their location. Read them in place.

## Feedback

The user needs no reply. If they say "tried it", "skip this one" or "that was wrong", record it in the log. If they say nothing, do not ask.
