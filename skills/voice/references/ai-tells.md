# Default list of AI tells to remove

These are removed from every draft unless the user has said otherwise in `preferences.md`. The user can add or remove items at any time.

## Punctuation and shape
- **Em dashes.** Use a period, comma or colon instead. This is the most common tell.
- **"Not X, but Y" and "It's not X, it's Y" constructions.** State Y directly.
- **Rule of three lists** used for rhythm rather than because there are three things.
- **Rhetorical questions** used as openers ("What if I told you...?").
- **Colon-and-reveal headlines** ("Here is the thing:").
- **Stacked short fragments for drama.** ("Fast. Simple. Done.")
- **Bold or italic emphasis scattered through plain text.**
- **Emoji** and decorative symbols, unless the user uses them.

## Superlatives and hype
- Best, greatest, incredible, amazing, game-changing, revolutionary, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, world-class, seamless, unparalleled, transformative.
- "Truly", "really", "absolutely", "incredibly", "deeply" used as intensifiers.
- Claims without evidence ("the most powerful way to...").

## Buzzwords
- Delve, leverage (as a verb), unlock, unleash, elevate, empower, harness, navigate (the landscape), foster, streamline, supercharge, robust, holistic, synergy, ecosystem (when plain words work), journey, tapestry, realm, landscape, paradigm.
- "In today's fast-paced world", "in the ever-evolving landscape of".

## Openers and throat-clearing
- "I hope this email finds you well", "I wanted to reach out", "I'm excited to share", "I am thrilled to announce".
- "Great question!", "Absolutely!", "Certainly!", "Of course!"
- Restating the request before answering.

## Closers
- "Let me know if you have any questions", "Feel free to reach out", "Looking forward to hearing from you" when they add nothing.
- "In conclusion", "To sum up", "Ultimately", "At the end of the day".
- A summary paragraph that repeats the message.

## Hedging and filler
- "I think", "it seems", "perhaps", "might be worth considering", "it's important to note that", "it's worth mentioning".
- "Moving forward", "going forward" as padding.
- Transition stacks ("Moreover", "Furthermore", "Additionally") at the start of consecutive sentences.

## Style
- Passive voice where an active sentence is clearer.
- Vague claims ("many people", "studies show") with no source.
- Every paragraph the same length.
- Perfectly balanced "on one hand, on the other hand" when the user has a view.

## How to handle a match
Rewrite the sentence in plain words, as the user would say it. If the sentence adds nothing once the tell is removed, delete it.
