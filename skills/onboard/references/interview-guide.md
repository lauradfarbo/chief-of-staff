# Interview guide

Question bank for the onboard skill. Pick from it. Skip anything the read-first draft already answered. Ask at most two questions per message.

## If a career file exists

A Career Asset File (from the career-foundation skill) answers much of Round 1 and the Voice section. Confirm instead of asking: current focus and target audience (Snapshot), current role (Timeline), voice and brand notes, and words to avoid. Ask only what it leaves out, mainly this quarter's outcomes, always-surface people and how the user works day to day (Rounds 2 and 3).

## Round 1: Role and outcomes

1. What do you own? One or two sentences on your role and who you work with.
2. What are the three outcomes that matter most to you this quarter? (Give a rough example if they stall: "Close the Q1 launch", "Hire two senior people", "Get the pipeline to X".)
3. Who should I always surface, even if the message looks minor? (Boss, key customer, partner, spouse, anyone.)
4. What are you saying no to right now? (Meetings, projects, request types. This helps me filter.)
5. Any dates coming up that everything is steering toward? (Launches, reviews, deadlines.)

## Round 2: How they work

6. Which recurring meetings need prep from you? What should the prep contain?
7. Which numbers do you check, and where do they come from? (Metric, source, how often.)
8. How much should I do alone versus ask first? Offer these, per area:
   - Draft only, never send (default)
   - Draft and ask before sending
   - Do it and tell me afterward (only for things they name)
9. What do you hate in a briefing or a draft? (Length, jargon, tables, emoji, certain phrases.)
10. What do you wish you could hand off entirely? (This seeds the skill-spotter.)

## Round 3: Setup

11. Which tool do you use for each category? (Only ask for categories not already found.)
12. Where should the briefing land? (A chat message to yourself, a doc, your inbox, a canvas.)
13. Which days and what time? Default is business days, 8:00 in your time zone.
14. How long should the list sections be? Default is 5 items per section. Anything beyond that is counted and saved to the full note, never hidden.
15. The mode question. Use the exact wording in the onboard skill.

## Voice

16. Show 3 to 5 voice observations from their sent messages and ask which are right.
17. Which of these do you dislike? Pre-check the common AI tells (see the voice skill) and let them uncheck or add to the list.
18. How do you sign off, and how formal are you with execs versus peers?

## Optional add-on

19. Last, mention that a Friday leadership note exists, off by default, and say how to turn it on ("start leadership notes"). Do not explain it further unless asked.

## Wrap-up

Playback in half a page. Ask what is wrong or missing. Then save and run the first win.
